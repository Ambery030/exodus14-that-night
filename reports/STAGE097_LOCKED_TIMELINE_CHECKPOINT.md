# Stage 0.97 — Locked Timeline / Mixed-Convoy Checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-20

## Status

**Conditional physical mechanism family found; full Exodus 14 compatibility is not yet demonstrated.**

Within the fixed synthetic map-informed world, an easterly gale followed by a
still-easterly strong-breeze tail, local low tide, and near-dawn wind cessation
can expose three firm route backbones, allow tens of thousands of mixed
traffic-equivalents to traverse, and then progressively remove all resolved
route elements near dawn.

This is a mechanism-family result, not a Late Bronze Age reconstruction and not
historical proof.  Egyptian pursuit, chariot degradation and fatality are not
included.

## Locks used

- Geometry: `MI_EAST_RELIEF_V1`; west-to-east geographic x-axis; eastern sandy
  relief beside western/lakeward low storage.
- Wind direction: true east to west, fixed at 180 degrees from positive x.
- Route length: 6.8 km synthetic crossing path.
- Firm routes: three resolved traffic backbones with declared usable widths
  60, 45 and 30 m; exact ridge geometry is synthetic.
- Mud/sabkha: zero traffic capacity.
- Tide: Port-Said-scale 0.15 m amplitude / 0.30 m range; external low-water
  phase fixed at 03:34 as a reference hypothesis, not an ancient prediction.
- Relative initial stage: -0.20 m sensitivity value; not an ancient Ballah
  water-level estimate.
- Central mixed mobility: 0.60 m/s and 0.30 mixed traffic-equivalents per
  metre-second; cargo/livestock effects are represented as convoy penalties,
  not separately reconstructed counts.

## Balanced candidate

The least severe well-performing tested profile is approximately:

- 16:00 wind begins;
- 17:00 reaches 19.0 m/s;
- 17:00–22:00 holds 19.0 m/s;
- 22:00–01:00 decays to 12.0 m/s;
- 01:00–04:00 holds a 12.0 m/s easterly tail;
- 04:00–04:30 decays to calm.

This is about five hours of gale-force peak, three hours of transition, three
hours of strong-breeze tail and a half-hour final cessation.  People are not
allowed to enter during the peak gale; the central mixed class becomes wind-
operable only after the wind falls below 13 m/s.

the event chain with less wind-operability stress and earlier closure.

## Ridge-count result

Three hydrodynamically resolved backbones are sufficient for the balanced
candidate family.  Adding unverified sub-grid fine ridges increases traffic
capacity but also leaves synthetic route elements open too long.  In the
diagnostic 20.5 m/s / 02:30-stop world, three, six and ten declared ridges
carried about 32,400, 46,800 and 55,440 central equivalents respectively, but
the six- and ten-ridge cases did not fully close their sub-grid route elements
within the diagnostic window.  They are capacity upper bounds, not preferred
physical solutions.

The result therefore supports **three resolved route backbones in the present
toy world**, not “ancient Ballah had exactly three ridges.”

## What “43,200” means

It is not 43,200 people plus animals and baggage.  It is the completed demand
of a central mixed-convoy surrogate after speed and specific-flow penalties for
people, stock and cargo.  Cargo carried by people or pack animals does not form
an independent head count.  A defensible people/animal split requires an
explicit convoy manifest and class-specific width/flow coefficients; those are
not presently evidenced.

The tested bounds are:

- urgent/light mixed upper: substantially higher capacity;
- central people/stock/cargo: about 43,200 equivalents in the preferred case;
- heavily burdened 0.45 m/s convoy: zero completed by 05:30.

Thus the current physical statement is “tens of thousands of mixed traffic-
equivalents,” not a claim for 600,000 people or a particular `eleph` reading.

## Remaining hard gaps

1. The peak 19 m/s wind remains severe for an ancient camp.  The model prevents
   convoy entry during the gale, but does not prove tent, loose-object or herd
   operability during the peak.
2. The 6.8 km route, -0.20 m stage, ridge elevations/widths and inlet profile
   are synthetic mechanism parameters.
3. Declared 30–60 m traffic widths are not hydrodynamically resolved at the
   500 m x-grid spacing; capacity is a transport-layer assumption.
4. The 03:34 tide is a locked modern reference phase, not an ancient date-specific
   prediction.
5. The return is progressive closure, not a demonstrated lethal wave.
6. Egyptian entry, pursuit timing, chariot degradation, attempted retreat and
   inundation remain Stage B.

## Decision

Proceed to Stage B only with the world frozen.  Do not alter geometry, tide,
wind or Israelite schedule to make the Egyptians fail.  The next test is whether
the same world allows Egyptian entry after the Israelites, produces mobility
degradation before major closure, and prevents successful retreat.
